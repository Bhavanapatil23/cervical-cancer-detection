The HPV vaccine is one of the most effective ways to prevent cervical cancer. Set a reminder for your upcoming dose.

Dose 1

First dose at any time. Recommended for ages 9–45.

Dose 2

1–2 months after Dose 1. Completes protection for ages 9–14 (2-dose schedule).

Dose 3

📌 Key Facts About HPV Vaccine

🛡️

Protects against HPV types 16 & 18, which cause ~70% of cervical cancers.

👧

Most effective when given before first sexual exposure (ages 9–12).

✅

Safe and approved by WHO, FDA, and major health authorities worldwide.

📅

Full immunization takes 2–3 doses over 6 months — don't miss your dose!
